# Shrikrrit Bhatter

Economics and Quantitative Finance graduate (Singapore Management University) working on macro research: how economic data is constructed, how central banks react to it, and what it means for rates, FX and equity markets. Previously on the macro research and strategy team at Dymon Asia Capital.

## Macro research

| Project | Question | Main finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [us-payrolls-divergence](https://github.com/Shrikrrit/us-payrolls-divergence) | Is the US payroll print overstating labour demand? | First prints are revised down in slowdowns through the annual benchmark, but the bias is too weak to trade in real time, and most monthly prints are noise against a 15k to 87k breakeven. |
| [fed-reaction-function](https://github.com/Shrikrrit/fed-reaction-function) | How does the Fed set rates, and where is policy against a Taylor rule? | With rate smoothing and the zero lower bound handled, the long-run inflation response is 1.67. Policy sits 2.7pp below the Taylor prescription. |

| [garch-volatility](https://github.com/Shrikrrit/garch-volatility) | Does GARCH beat a rolling window at forecasting equity volatility? | Not significantly out of sample in 2023 to 2024 (Diebold-Mariano p = 0.25). |
